# Code of conduct

AttentionOS adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, chat and events, and when someone represents the project in public.

## In short

- Be welcoming and patient. Many people here are business owners, not developers, and every question is a fair one.
- Be kind and constructive. Critique ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexualised language or imagery, personal attacks, trolling, or publishing anyone's private information.
- Respect privacy. Never post anyone's personal or client data, including in screenshots and logs.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainers through [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting form](https://github.com/evanbaker315/AttentionOS/security/advisories/new) (mark it as a conduct report), or contact a maintainer directly.

All reports are reviewed promptly and handled in confidence. Maintainers follow the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
