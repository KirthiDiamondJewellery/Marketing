# Shoot: pop-up presenter

**Framing.** Vertical (9:16), chest-up, face centred. You appear in a small window, so the face matters more than the background: keep it simple and uncluttered.

**Light and sound.** Even light on your face; the take is also the voiceover, so sound quality matters most. Quiet room, mic close, no echo.

**What to film.**
- The whole script in one take to camera, sentence by sentence. It plays under the visuals.
- Mark the lines that carry your opinion or the turn: deliver them straight to the lens. Those become the pop-ups.
- Say "cut that" to drop a take; the last full take wins.

**B-roll list.**
- A visual for each fact or step: a screen recording, a screenshot or a photo you own (3 to 6 s each).
- At most 8 stills and 6 loops. Note where each one came from and its license.
- Nothing with someone else's private details, logos you can't use, or footage you don't have the rights to.
