# Shoot: split-screen demo

**Framing (you).** Vertical (9:16), chest-up, eyes about a third from the top. Only the bottom half of the frame shows you, so keep your face centred and your hands low.

**Light and sound.** Soft light on your face, no bright window behind you. Quiet room, mic close.

**Screen recording.**
- Record at 1080p or higher. Zoom the browser or app to 125 to 150% so text reads on a phone.
- Use a large cursor and move it slowly to what you are talking about.
- Close notifications and tabs. Hide anything private: names, emails, client or deal details, folder names. If in doubt, leave it out.
- Record a few extra seconds before and after each step.

**What to film.**
- Hook first, face to camera. Then talk through the steps while the recording plays (or in a second take, in the same order).
- Say "cut that" to drop a take; the last full take of a line wins.

**B-roll list.**
- One recording per step or result (3 to 6 s each).
- A screenshot of each source you cite, with the page's address visible.
