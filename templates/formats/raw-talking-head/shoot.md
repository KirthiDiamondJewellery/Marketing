# Shoot: raw talking head

**Framing.** Vertical (9:16), phone at eye level, chest-up. Leave a little space above your head; the top of the frame is covered by the app's UI. Look at the lens, not the screen.

**Light.** Face a window or a soft light. No bright lamp or window behind you. Turn off overhead lights that hit the top of your head.

**Sound.** Quiet room, phone or lav mic within arm's length. Soft furnishings help; bare rooms echo.

**What to film.**
- Say the hook first, as one sentence, then pause a beat. Redo it as many times as you like: the last full take wins.
- Deliver each point as a full sentence. If you flub, pause and say the line again from the start.
- To drop something, say "cut that" and carry on. The editor removes the take before it.
- End on the call to action, then hold still for one second.

**B-roll.** None needed.
