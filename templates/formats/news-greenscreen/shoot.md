# Shoot: news green screen

**Framing.** Vertical (9:16), waist-up, centred, with space above your head. You will sit in the lower half of the final frame, so keep gestures in front of your body.

**Background.** A green screen, evenly lit with no creases or shadows, gives the cleanest cut-out. No green screen? A plain, evenly lit wall works: you will appear in a rounded window instead. Don't wear green (or the background colour).

**Light and sound.** Light your face and the background separately if you can; avoid shadows on the backdrop. Quiet room, mic close.

**What to film.**
- One line on what happened (the hook). Then what it means for your viewer, and what they should do.
- Mention each source by name as you go, so the editor can put it on screen at that moment.
- Say "cut that" to drop a take; the last full take wins.
- Film within 24 hours of the story.

**B-roll list.**
- A screenshot of each article, post or official page you mention (full page, address bar visible).
- The one number that tells the story, written down with where it comes from.
