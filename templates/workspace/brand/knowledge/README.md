# Knowledge

Optional deeper documents the agents can read: methods you teach, your process in detail, a pricing guide, research you trust, notes from a book you base your work on. Markdown files (`.md`) only.

## Authority order

When two sources disagree, agents follow the one higher on this list:

1. `brand/lessons.md`: your latest corrections always win.
2. `brand/offers.md` and `brand/proof-inventory.yaml`: what you sell and what you can prove.
3. `brand/voice.md`, `brand/icp.md`, `brand/pillars.md`, `brand/house-style.md`.
4. Files in this folder, in the order you list them below.
5. General knowledge from the model, used only when nothing above covers it.
