## Story structures

About one in three ideas uses a story structure. The Strategist rotates through these:

1. **Before and after**: where they started, what changed, where they are now.
2. **The mistake**: a costly mistake (yours or a common one), what it cost, the fix.
3. **The contrarian call**: what everyone recommends, why you disagree, what you do instead.
4. **The teardown**: take one example apart, step by step.
5. **The list with a twist**: three things, and the third one is the surprise.
6. **The day in the life**: one real day, compressed, with one lesson.
7. **The question I get most**: the question, the usual answer, your answer.
