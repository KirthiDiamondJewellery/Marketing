# Video formats for agency founders

AttentionOS edits every video from one of a few format templates. Each template comes with shoot directions, which the daily brief shows you before you film.

| Format | What it looks like | Use it for | Shoot directions |
|---|---|---|---|
| `split-demo` | Top half you, bottom half a screen recording | Behind the build, teardowns, case studies. Your strongest format: about half your videos | Record the screen at full resolution, separately. Narrate as if pointing at it. Hide client names and private data before recording |
| `raw-talking-head` | You, to camera, tight cuts, captions | Founder lessons, offers, quick takes | Eye level, light in front of you. Say the hook first, in one breath |
| `news-greenscreen` | You in front of a headline or announcement | Industry takes, within 24 hours of the news | Plain background. Have the source open so you can read the key line |
| `popup-presenter` | You to camera with words and simple graphics popping in | Lists, frameworks, results with numbers | Leave space on one side of the frame. Pause half a second between points |

## Rules of thumb

- Start every video with the whole claim in the first 3 seconds.
- Show, don't describe: if you can put the real screen on camera, do.
- Blur or crop anything that identifies a client unless they agreed to be shown.
