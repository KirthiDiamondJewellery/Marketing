# Quotas are per week. funnelStage: tofu (reach), mofu (trust), bofu (sale).
lanes:
  - id: case-study
    name: Case study
    weeklyQuota: 1
    funnelStage: bofu
    description: A client result, with the problem, what you built and the outcome. Numbers only from proof-inventory.yaml; no client names without consent.
  - id: behind-the-build
    name: Behind the build
    weeklyQuota: 2
    funnelStage: mofu
    description: Show the real work on screen. A dashboard, a workflow, a draft, a decision.
  - id: industry-take
    name: Industry take
    weeklyQuota: 2
    funnelStage: tofu
    description: A clear opinion on news, a platform change or a trend your buyers are hearing about. Film within 24 hours.
  - id: teardown
    name: Teardown
    weeklyQuota: 1
    funnelStage: tofu
    description: Take apart one public example (an ad, a landing page, a funnel) and show what works and what doesn't.
  - id: founder-lesson
    name: Founder lesson
    weeklyQuota: 2
    funnelStage: mofu
    description: Something running the agency taught you about clients, pricing, hiring or delivery.
  - id: offer
    name: Offer
    weeklyQuota: 1
    funnelStage: bofu
    description: Who you help, the result, and the one next step (comment a keyword or book a call).

## Angle bank

- **The hidden cost**: what the client was losing before you fixed it.
- **We tried it so you don't have to**: a tool, tactic or platform feature, tested for real.
- **The boring thing that works**: an unglamorous habit behind your best results.
- **What clients ask for versus what they need**: and how you bridge the gap.
- **Pricing honesty**: how you price, and what cheap really costs.
- **The build, start to finish**: one project compressed into 60 seconds.

## Story structures

About one in three ideas uses one of these. The Strategist rotates through them.

1. **Before and after**: the client's starting point, the build, the result.
2. **The mistake**: something that went wrong on a project, what it cost, what you changed.
3. **The contrarian call**: the industry consensus, why you disagree, what you do instead.
4. **The teardown**: one public example, taken apart step by step.
5. **Three things, one surprise**: a list of three where the third breaks the pattern.
6. **The day in the life**: a real day at the agency with one lesson.
7. **The question I get most**: the question, the usual answer, your answer.
