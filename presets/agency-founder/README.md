# Preset: agency founder

A starting point for founders of service agencies who sell projects and retainers to other businesses.

## What it sets up

| File | Goes to | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `pillars.md` | `brand/pillars.md` | Six lanes with weekly quotas (9 videos a week), angle bank, story structures, formats per lane |
| `sources.yaml` | `brand/sources.yaml` | Generic marketing, platform and AI news searches plus community sources |
| `cadence.yaml` | `brand/knowledge/` | Posts per week and time slots per platform, with how strong the evidence is |
| `cta.yaml` | `brand/knowledge/` | Call to action per funnel stage, keyword rules, lead magnet ideas |
| `formats.md` | `brand/knowledge/` | Which video format to use when, and how to film it |
| `preset.json` | (metadata) | Funnel mix 50/30/20, north star: leads per week |

## Why this mix

Agency buyers want to see that you can do the work. So the preset leans on screen-based formats (behind the build, teardowns) and adds more LinkedIn and X text posts, where business buyers read. Industry takes keep you visible to new people between case studies.

## Make it yours

Everything here is a default. After `/attention:init`, rename lanes to match your words, change quotas to fit your filming time, and add news searches for your speciality to `brand/sources.yaml`. The learn module will suggest changes once it has about 20 of your posts to measure.
