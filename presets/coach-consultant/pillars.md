## Angle bank

Starting angles that work for most coaches and consultants. Keep the ones that fit and add your own.

- **The expensive mistake**: what your clients got wrong before they met you, and what it cost.
- **The honest answer**: the thing others in your niche won't say out loud.
- **The shortcut that isn't**: why the quick fix makes things slower.
- **The small hinge**: one small change that moved a big result.
- **The question behind the question**: what people ask versus what they actually need to know.
- **Your process, shown**: one step of how you work, filmed as it happens.

## Story structures

About one in three ideas uses one of these. The Strategist rotates through them.

1. **Before and after**: where the client started, the turning point, where they are now.
2. **The mistake**: a common costly mistake, what it cost, the fix.
3. **The contrarian call**: what everyone recommends, why you disagree, what you do instead.
4. **The teardown**: take one example apart, step by step.
5. **Three things, one surprise**: a list of three where the third breaks the pattern.
6. **The client call**: a (consented or anonymised) moment from a session and what it taught.
7. **The question I get most**: the question, the usual answer, your answer.
