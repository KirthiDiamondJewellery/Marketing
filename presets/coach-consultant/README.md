# Preset: coach or consultant

A starting point for coaches, consultants and owner-led service businesses who sell expertise through calls, programs or retainers.

## What it sets up

| File | Goes to | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `pillars.md` | `brand/pillars.md` | Six lanes with weekly quotas (8 videos a week), angle bank, story structures, formats per lane |
| `sources.yaml` | `brand/sources.yaml` | Generic news and community sources for business audiences |
| `cadence.yaml` | `brand/knowledge/` | Posts per week and time slots per platform, with how strong the evidence is |
| `cta.yaml` | `brand/knowledge/` | Call to action per funnel stage, keyword rules, lead magnet ideas |
| `formats.md` | `brand/knowledge/` | Which video format to use when, and how to film it |
| `preset.json` | (metadata) | Funnel mix 45/35/20, north star: booked calls per week |

## Why this mix

Coaching and consulting are considered purchases. People watch for weeks before they book. So the mix leans a little more on trust (buyer questions, frameworks) than the default, and the north star is booked calls rather than followers.

## Make it yours

Everything here is a default. After `/attention:init`, rename lanes to match your words, change quotas to fit your filming time, and add news searches for your niche to `brand/sources.yaml`. The learn module will suggest changes once it has about 20 of your posts to measure.
