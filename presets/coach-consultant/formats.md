# Video formats for coaches and consultants

AttentionOS edits every video from one of a few format templates. Each template comes with shoot directions, which the daily brief shows you before you film.

| Format | What it looks like | Use it for | Shoot directions |
|---|---|---|---|
| `raw-talking-head` | You, to camera, tight cuts, captions | Buyer questions, myth busts, offers. The workhorse: about half your videos | Eye level, window light in front of you, phone at arm's length or on a tripod. Say the hook first, in one breath |
| `popup-presenter` | You to camera, with key words and simple graphics popping in beside you | Frameworks, client wins with numbers, lists | Leave a little space on one side of the frame. Pause half a second between points |
| `split-demo` | Top half you, bottom half a screen recording or document | Showing a tool, a template or a worked example | Record the screen separately; narrate as if pointing at it |
| `news-greenscreen` | You in front of a headline or article screenshot | News takes, within 24 hours of the story | Plain background behind you. Have the headline open so you can read the key line |

## Rules of thumb

- Start every video with the whole claim in the first 3 seconds.
- One idea per video. If you have two, that's two videos.
- Film a full week in one session: change your shirt between lanes so the week doesn't look like one day.
