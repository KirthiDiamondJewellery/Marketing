# Presets

A preset gives a kind of business a sensible starting point: content lanes with weekly quotas, a funnel mix, a call-to-action model, posting cadence, trend sources and video formats. `PRESET=<id>` in the stack's `.env` (or `attentionos create my-brand --preset <id>`) copies one into a new workspace's Brand Kit, and you change anything you like afterwards.

| Preset | For | North star |
|---|---|---|
| [`coach-consultant`](coach-consultant/) | Coaches, consultants and owner-led service businesses | Booked calls per week |
| [`agency-founder`](agency-founder/) | Founders of marketing, creative, development and automation agencies | Leads per week |

More presets (local service, creator-educator, ecommerce brand, B2B SaaS) are planned after v0.1. Contributions are welcome: copy an existing folder, keep everything generic (no real names, brands or accounts), and mark how strong the evidence is behind each cadence rule.
